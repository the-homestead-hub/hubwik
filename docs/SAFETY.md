# Content safety

HubWīk teaches household growing. It is not medical advice and not a poison
control service. Wrong copy can still hurt someone.

## Elevated review

A change needs elevated review when it speaks to any of:

- edibility or “safe to eat”
- toxicity, look-alikes, or wild collection
- allergens
- pesticides, herbicides, or other chemical controls
- manure, compost teas, or human/animal waste
- preservation, canning, fermenting, or storage that can grow pathogens
- children
- pets
- pregnancy

The pull request must then record:

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Jurisdiction | Where the claim is meant to hold (for example England and Wales, 2026). Growing law and food law differ. |
| Source date | When the cited source was current. |
| Reviewer | A named person who read the claim. The contributor may not be the only reviewer on an elevated change. |

The lead maintainer may require a second reader or may refuse the claim.

## What we will not publish

- Foraging or wild-identification advice that could be confused with a
  catalogue variety
- Dosage, cure, or treatment claims
- Instructions that tell a reader to ignore a product label
- Partner or grower names attached to a failure or an accusation
- Photographs without a completed provenance row

## Working recommendations

A `working_recommendation` or `live_treatise` claim is weaker than a measured
`thh_observation`. Say so in the record. Incomplete crops must keep
`status: incomplete` until yield and the other complete-crop fields are filled
from a source that can bear them.

## Incidents

If published copy may cause harm, open a security-style private report as
[SECURITY.md](../SECURITY.md) describes, or mail hello@thehomesteadhub.co.uk
with the subject `HubWīk safety`. We will treat a live safety error as a
hotfix, not as a discussion thread.
