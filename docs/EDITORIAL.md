# HubWīk editorial charter · v0.1

These statements are the public operating law of the book. Grow pages stay
instruction. This file is the law those pages obey.

## Statements

**HW-01 · One body.** HubWīk is a child wick of The Homestead Hub. Do not stand
up a second encyclopaedia brand, academy, or knowledge house.

**HW-02 · Treatises stay open.** A grow treatise is never titled. HTTP 402 on a
cite-face URL is a forfeited citation. Humans pay at a shop till. Agents may
meet a stile only for scarce computation.

**HW-03 · Title the scarce thing.** A stile’s job is a planner, a crop-record
dump, or later observation-conditioned recommendations — not a basil page
fetch. Settlement is unpublished until a separate, private cutover. The lockup
never says 402.

**HW-04 · One ontology line.** Eighteen varieties in the knowledge catalogue.
Sixteen ship in the Four by Four. Twelve ship in the Three by Four. Any
variety, £2.99. State it once on the hall. Then the satellites stay about the
crop. Catalogue prices in the public profile are notes, not a live till feed.

**HW-05 · Explain the wick once.** Lore and the HubWīk explainer may name the
architecture. A grow page teaches the crop. If a grow page is talking about
HubWīk, cut it.

**HW-06 · Provenance on load-bearing numbers.** A first-cut week, a temperature
floor, or a sow depth carries a kind: `thh_observation`, `thh_trial`,
`supplier_spec`, `horticultural_consensus`, `external_study`,
`working_recommendation`, or `live_treatise`. Say what was measured, what a
paper found, and what has not been tested.

**HW-07 · Show what. Never who.** Public treatises never name a partner firm, a
grower, or an accusation geography. Partner rows stamp `partner_observation`
with conditions, not a logo. `partner_id` is internal and must never print.

**HW-08 · Record then page.** Files under `data/crops/` are canon for copy.
HubWīk URLs are the cite-face. A grow page is generated from the record. Never
by hand in `dist/`.

**HW-09 · Horizon stays behind the work.** Food-sovereignty, payment-protocol,
and information-architecture essays do not print on grow pages or kit
treatises. The spoken line is already on the shop: *Sow on the sill. Cut for
supper.*

**HW-10 · House protocol, quietly.** Write the method — pinch above a node,
succession, this medium, this timeline — without a trademark slap. The
practice should survive with the logo off.

**HW-11 · Sources mean citations.** Internal navigation is not a source list.
If the block only routes to the pack, the medium, and the hall, it is not a
citation. Cite the work you used.

**HW-12 · The unseen surface matches the seen.** Reconciliation notes, build
process sentences, and truncated infobox fields do not ship.

## Open versus titled

| Surface | Posture |
|---|---|
| Grow treatise, hall, shop-explained | Open. Machine-legible. HowTo / FAQ / Article. |
| Live price, stock, variant feeds | Behind a till. Not a scrapeable catalogue in this repository. |
| Planner; crop-record JSON dump; observation-conditioned recommendations | Titled when a private settlement service opens. Humans still buy at a shop. |
| Stile Worker | Health check. Never a citation URL. |

## What this charter does not contain

Distribution strategy, private operations tooling, and partner-recruitment
order of approach stay out of the public book. Observation *row shape* is
public; see [OBSERVATION.md](OBSERVATION.md).
