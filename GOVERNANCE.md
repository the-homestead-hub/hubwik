# Governance

HubWīk is maintained by a person, not a committee.

## Lead maintainer

**Shiven** is the lead maintainer and the final merge and release authority.
That includes licence exceptions, trademark questions, safety overrides, and
whether a contribution enters the public book.

GitHub handle for code ownership: `@n3v1h5`.

## How decisions are made

Editorial law is [docs/EDITORIAL.md](docs/EDITORIAL.md). Safety law is
[docs/SAFETY.md](docs/SAFETY.md). The rights boundary is
[docs/BOUNDARY.md](docs/BOUNDARY.md).

A pull request that passes automated checks still needs a human read. Passing
CI is not a merge right.

There are no elections and no standing committee. Maintainers are added only
after sustained, careful contributions, by the lead maintainer.

## Releases

Public versions are tagged on this repository. Private shops and dashboards
should pin a tag or commit. They must not become a second editable canon.

## Conduct

[CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) applies to issues, pull requests, and
other project spaces. The lead maintainer enforces it.
