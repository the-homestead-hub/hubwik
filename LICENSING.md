# Licensing

HubWīk uses two licences. The split is by kind of file, not by contributor.

| Kind | Licence | Typical paths |
|---|---|---|
| Software | Apache-2.0 | `src/`, `tests/`, `adapters/`, `examples/`, `.github/`, `pyproject.toml` |
| Original prose and data | CC BY 4.0 | `content/`, `data/`, `docs/`, community policy markdown at the root |
| Generated output | same as its inputs | `dist/` is never canon. Do not contribute generated HTML or Liquid as source. |

Full texts live in `LICENSES/Apache-2.0.txt` and `LICENSES/CC-BY-4.0.txt`.
Machine-readable SPDX mappings live in `REUSE.toml`.

## What you may do

You may use, modify, and redistribute the compiler under Apache-2.0.

You may share and adapt the treatises and crop records under CC BY 4.0, provided
you give attribution as requested below.

A citation is not a copyright licence. Do not import text, tables, or images
from a third party unless that party’s licence is recorded in
[docs/PROVENANCE.md](docs/PROVENANCE.md) and the import is compatible with
CC BY 4.0.

## Attribution

For software, retain copyright, licence, and notice files as Apache-2.0 requires.

For prose and data, a reasonable attribution is:

> HubWīk crop records and treatises by The Homestead Hub and contributors,
> [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

Name individual authors when a file records them. Mark your modifications.

## Images

This repository starts without images. An image is accepted only when
[docs/PROVENANCE.md](docs/PROVENANCE.md) records author, source, exact licence,
required attribution, and modification status.
