# Four by Four

The Four by Four is the larger stack: four quad planters, sixteen five-litre positions, sixteen of the eighteen catalogue varieties in the box. Dill and pea have treatises; they are not in this kit. Same soil-and-open-seeds system as the Three by Four. Three by Four is the beginner kit.

<p>Sixteen positions are still a courtyard or a wide sill, not a smallholding. They are the larger start of the same practice as the Three by Four: leaves and herbs you stop buying because you cut them here. The planter is still the pot.</p>
