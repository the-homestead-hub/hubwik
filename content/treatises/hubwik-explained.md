# Hubwik

Hubwik is the wick of The Homestead Hub: a specialised out-farm whose product is knowledge. Spoken Hub Wick. Typed hubwik. Alias hubwick. It is a child of the shop, not a second house, and not a chatbot.

## Name

<p>Spoken <strong>Hub Wick</strong>. Typed <strong>hubwik</strong>. The alias <strong>hubwick</strong> is the same place. The lockup is Hub with a raised <em>Wīk</em> — macron on the <em>i</em>, Montserrat with latin-ext, CSS, not a picture of a logo unless the glass nav fails.</p>
<p>The coinage is not Farmwiki and not a general encyclopaedia brand. The Homestead Hub is the shop. Hubwik is the out-farm of that shop. Its product is knowledge that can be stood up next to a bag of seed.</p>

## Wick

<p>Old English <em>wīc</em> (from Latin <em>vicus</em>) names a specialised settlement: a dairy farm, a salt-works, a trading place dependent on somewhere else. Place-names in <em>-wick</em> and <em>-wich</em> keep the sense. Ekwall glossed it as dwelling, village, town, street, farm — especially a dairy-farm. Richard Coates stresses the specialised, non-subsistence place: an out-farm, not the whole manor.</p>
<p>That is the metaphor. The Hub is the working place. The wick is the specialised out-farm whose crop is standing knowledge. A wick is also the cord that draws fuel to a flame; the pun is allowed, and it is secondary to the Old English.</p>

## Child of the shop

<p>Hubwik is not a second website, not a second brand, and not a personality. Brand direction is product-led. Don may explain a crop; Don does not host the encyclopaedia. Founders may appear when their knowledge helps; neither is the permanent face of the book.</p>
<p>Canonical hall: <a href="/pages/hubwik">/pages/hubwik</a>. Seed rooms sit at <code>/pages/grow-…</code> and open the Seed Pack on that variety. Product treatises sit in the same book and open the live till. The titled room is a stile in this book, not a new house.</p>

## What is cited

<p>Answer engines cite stable URLs that carry a claim, a number, and schema. They do not cite a search box. Hubwik is the public face of the quotable set the public cite-set names: eighteen varieties, and the live product URLs (kits, coco, lamp, seed pack). Each treatise has a cite-face — lede, infobox, HowTo or FAQ, sources — and a deep face on the same URL for people who stay.</p>
<p>The hall is the contents of the book. It is not a featured-article magazine, not a SKU grid, and not a chat chrome. Query-shaped retrieval is what engines do <em>off-site</em> when they quote these pages.</p>

## Feorm under the floorboards

<p>Old English <em>feorm</em> is food-rent, provisioning, the render that kept a place. HTTP 402 Payment Required was reserved in the status table and left unused. The x402 protocol puts a payment in that status so an agent can pay a resource in the same round-trip a browser uses for a page.</p>
<p>On Hubwik that idea is a stile, not a masthead. See <a href="/pages/titled">Titled</a>. Humans keep a wallet-less till-path in the shop. The lockup never says 402. Agents meet HTTP 402 at <a href="https://stile.thehomesteadhub.co.uk/">stile.thehomesteadhub.co.uk</a>. Settlement is not open.</p>

## What it is not

<p>Not a second house. Not Farmwiki. Not anyone-edits Wikipedia — household knowledge is titled; the crowd does not overwrite the treatise. Not a social network. Not a Perplexity skin. Helpful like a good thread; original like a shop that grows the thing it sells.</p>
