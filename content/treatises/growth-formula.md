# Growth Formula

Growth Formula is a mineral nutrient for coco coir: NPK 4-2-6, one hundred millilitres, pump bottle. Shake before use. Two pumps per litre. Use with every watering. £7.99 a bottle; two £13.99; three £19.99; then £5 a further bottle. Not packed inside Three by Four or Four by Four.

## The bottle

<p>Live on the shop as <a href="/products/growth-formula">Growth Formula</a>. Shake before use. Its own Range page — not an add-on SKU inside the kits. The quantity ladder is this page's: £7.99, £13.99, £19.99, then £5 a further bottle. That is not the Seed Pack ladder and not the One by One curve.</p>

## Why a mineral feed on coco

<p>Coco coir holds water and air. It does not come loaded like a peat compost. The Coco Soil bag is pre-buffered with calcium and magnesium and charged; Growth Formula is the ongoing mineral feed once the plant is drinking. NPK 4-2-6 is the ratio on this bottle. THH has not published a house trial of this feed against another brand. The claim is the live bottle, the dose, and that it is sold for coco.</p>

## How to use

<ol>
<li>Shake the bottle.</li>
<li>Two pumps per litre of water.</li>
<li>Use with every watering.</li>
</ol>
<p>Coco wants a regular weak feed more than a rare strong one. Do not invent a third dose on this page.</p>
